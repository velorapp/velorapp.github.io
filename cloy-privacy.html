Cloy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Cloy is an iPhone app that helps you quit sugar by seeing through what it promises. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Your answers, your plan, your check-ins and your slips stay on your iPhone. We never receive what you eat, when sugar finds you, why you want to stop or how you feel. To run the app we use Firebase (anonymous usage events and crash reports, which you can turn off) and RevenueCat (subscription records). Apple handles payments. There is no advertising, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Data on your iPhone

Your answers (your favorite sweets, the times of day sugar finds you, what sets it off, the things you've told yourself about it, how much quitting matters to you and why, the plan for your hardest moment and anything you type in your own words), your answers to the two safety questions, the plan built from them and your progress (chapters read, your Last Bite, check-ins, slips, the times you asked for help when Cloy was talking, and your Party Mode and Holiday Shield plans) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's protected storage. They are never sent to us or to any service. The widgets read your progress from the same storage on your iPhone.

This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Health information

Cloy asks two safety questions: whether you take medicine for diabetes, and whether you have had support for an eating disorder. Your answers only change what the app shows you; they stay on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere, including in usage data. Cloy doesn't read or write Apple Health data.

The camera

The Ingredient X-Ray uses the camera to read the ingredients printed on a label. The text is read on your iPhone; pictures are not saved, and nothing from the camera is sent anywhere.

No account

Cloy has no sign-in and no account, and we keep no database about you. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so you can restore it on a new iPhone from the app. If we add features that need more, such as connecting with friends, we will update this policy before they launch.

Usage and crash data

Firebase Analytics (Google) receives events about how the app is used: which onboarding step you reach, when the subscription screen is shown or closed, trials, purchases and restores, when you finish a chapter or your Last Bite, when you open the help for a craving and when you finish it, when you check in or start fresh, when you use the Ingredient X-Ray and how many sugars and sweeteners it found, when you turn on Party Mode or Holiday Shield and how many days you protected, and when you open the app from a notification or a link. It also collects standard information such as your device model, iOS and app version, an app-instance identifier and your approximate region based on your IP address. These events never include your answers, your check-ins or anything you type.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports and error logs with similar device information, so we can find and fix problems.

Your choice. In the EU and the EEA, usage and crash data stay off until you say yes. In the UK and Switzerland the app tells you about them first, and you can turn them off. Everywhere, you can change your mind at any time in the app, under You > Share anonymous usage; while it's off, nothing is sent to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

We don't use the advertising identifier, don't ask to track you and don't use this data for advertising. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

To unlock Cloy Pro and to understand how subscriptions are doing, the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with a random ID that RevenueCat creates for the app on your iPhone. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email or your answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your iPhone: a heads-up at your sugar hours, the evening check-in, the plans you make in Party Mode and Holiday Shield, and one the day before a free trial ends. They are created on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

Deleting your data

In the app, You > Erase everything deletes everything Cloy stored on your iPhone and resets the random identifiers used for usage data and purchases. Deleting the app also removes the data on your iPhone. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account and can be restored. To delete the purchase records RevenueCat holds, write to velorappsupport@gmail.com with the order ID from Apple's receipt email, so we can find them. We'll confirm when it's done.

Children

Cloy is made for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
