Common questions

My steps don't show up.

Yuru reads your steps from Apple Health. Open the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps > Yuru, and turn on Steps, Walking + Running Distance and Flights Climbed. For steps to count while you walk, also open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps > Yuru and turn on Motion & Fitness.

Why is my count a little ahead of the Health app?

Health can take a few minutes to record a walk. Yuru adds the steps your iPhone has just counted on top of what Health already has, so the wolf walks with you in the moment. Once Health catches up the two agree, and no step is counted twice.

Does Yuru count steps from my Apple Watch?

Yes. Steps your Apple Watch records reach Yuru through Apple Health, and Health makes sure a step taken with both your Watch and your iPhone is counted once.

How does the weekly league work?

Each week you walk in a group of up to 30 walkers. Ten steps make one XP, up to a daily cap. The week turns over on Monday at 00:00 UTC: the top of the group moves up a league and the bottom moves down. Nobody drops out of the first league.

I'd rather not compete.

On the League tab, choose Walk without the league. Your journey, postcards and badges carry on as before, and your row comes off the week's board. You can come back whenever you like.

How do I add a friend?

On the League tab, share your name and code with a friend, or type theirs to add them. Friends see each other's name, wolf and weekly XP, never the step history.

Can I change my name?

Yes. Tap your own row on the league board and choose a new one. The short code after your name stays yours.

How does the free trial work?

The weekly plan starts with 3 days free for new subscribers. If notifications are on, Yuru reminds you before the trial ends. You won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. The yearly plan has no trial.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick Yuru. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all payments and refunds.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Yuru and tap Restore at the bottom of the Yuru Pro screen.

My notifications don't arrive.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications > Yuru and make sure notifications are allowed.

Where is my data?

Your step history and your journey stay on your iPhone and are included in your iCloud or computer backup. The league keeps the name you chose and your week's steps under an anonymous account. The privacy policy has the details, and how to have it deleted.

Is Yuru medical advice?

No. Yuru is a walking companion, not medical advice or treatment. If you have a health condition or haven't been active for a while, talk to your doctor before you change how much you walk.
