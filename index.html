FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is behind Velorapp?

Velorapp is the studio name of Mertcan Erbasi, an independent developer. Our apps appear on the App Store under that name.

Do your apps collect my data?

Our apps keep what you enter on your iPhone. We don't run accounts, ads or tracking. Each app has its own privacy policy that lists exactly what, if anything, leaves your device.

How do subscriptions and free trials work?

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. If a plan has a free trial, the app shows when it ends, and you aren't charged if you cancel before then. Plans renew automatically until you cancel.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

More detail is on our Subscriptions & Refunds page.

I have a new iPhone. How do I get my subscription back?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Restore purchases in its settings.

How can I reach you?

Use the Support page. You can also write to the address on our Support page.

Questions, ideas or a bug?

Tell us what happened and which app it was. We read every message.
