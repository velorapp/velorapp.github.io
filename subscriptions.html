Subscriptions & Refunds

Last updated: November 10, 2026

How subscriptions in Velorapp apps are billed, renewed, canceled and refunded.

Who sells the subscription

Subscriptions in our apps are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store, using the payment method on your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

Prices

Each app shows the price, the billing period and any free trial before you subscribe. Prices may differ by country and include any taxes that apply. If a price goes up, Apple tells you in advance and, where the law requires it, asks for your consent.

Free trials

If a plan includes a free trial, the app shows when the trial ends. Payment is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.

Canceling

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick the app. Most of our apps also have a Manage subscription button in their settings. After you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Because Apple processes all payments, refunds are decided by Apple under its policies and the law that applies to you. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds directly, but we're happy to help: write to the address on our Support page.

Restoring on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app and tap Restore purchases in its settings.

Family Sharing

Unless an app's App Store page says otherwise, our subscriptions are for the Apple Account that buys them and aren't shared through Family Sharing.
