Reckon

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Reckon is an iPhone app that turns your front camera into a mirror: you write the day’s vows on it in the morning and answer for them at night. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

The camera picture is shown on your screen and nowhere else: it is never recorded, saved or sent, and the app does not read it. Your vows and your days are stored on your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud. There is no account, no sign-up and no advertising, and the app doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. To run the app we use RevenueCat (subscription records) and TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage counts that never include what you write). Apple handles payments.

The camera

With your permission, Reckon uses the front camera only to show your reflection while the app is open. The picture is not recorded, not saved, not analysed and not sent anywhere, and no frame of it is read by the app. If you turn the camera off in your iPhone’s settings, the mirror simply stays dark.

Your vows and your days

What you write, the days you sign and close, and what you earn are stored on your iPhone, in Reckon’s own storage. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings.

Reckon also keeps a copy in your own iCloud, through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so that a new iPhone, or the app installed again, starts where you left off. That copy sits in your Apple Account; we can’t see it. You can stop it by turning off iCloud for Reckon in your iPhone’s iCloud settings.

Deleting the app deletes what Reckon stored on your iPhone.

Usage data

TelemetryDeck receives anonymous signals about how the app is used: which welcome step you reach, when the plans screen is shown or closed, trials, purchases and restores, when a day is signed or closed and how many vows were kept, when the fog is wiped, when you hold on at the wall and the ranks you reach. Each signal also carries technical details such as the app and iOS version, the device model and your language and region settings. Before a signal leaves your iPhone, the device’s identifier is replaced with a hashed value, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again on its servers, so we can’t tell who you are. Signals never include what you write, and nothing from the camera.

We don’t use the advertising identifier, don’t ask to track you and don’t use this data for advertising. See TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple’s privacy policy.

To unlock Reckon Pro and to understand how subscriptions are doing, the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with an anonymous ID it creates for your device. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email or anything you write. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your iPhone: a call in the morning, a question in the evening, one more if the day is still open, and one before a free trial ends. They are created on your device and never contain the words of your vows; nothing about them is sent to us.

Siri and Shortcuts

You can put a vow on the mirror by voice. What you say is handled by Apple under its own privacy terms, and the vow is stored on your iPhone like any other.

Where your data is kept

On your iPhone, in your own iCloud, and, for usage signals and purchase records, with TelemetryDeck (in the European Union) and RevenueCat, which may process data in the United States and other countries under their own privacy terms linked above.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Reckon stored on your iPhone; turning off iCloud for Reckon stops the iCloud copy. To have the purchase records RevenueCat holds deleted, write to velorappsupport@gmail.com. Usage signals can’t be traced back to you, so there is nothing in them to find. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account, where you can cancel it at any time.

Children

Reckon is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
