Yuru

Every step, together. A wolf walks as far as you do, sends a postcard from every stop and grows up as you go.

Coming soon to the App StoreHealth & FitnessiPhone · English · Español · Português · Türkçe

Your walks, turned into a journey

One number, one wolf and the road ahead. No ads, no busy charts.

A wolf that walks with you

Your steps move Yuru through the worlds, live while you walk. Level up and the wolf grows with you: pup, young, wolf and legend.

Postcards from every stop

Each world has ten stops. Every stop you reach sends a postcard to your album.

A weekly league

Walk in a group of up to 30 each week. The top walkers move up, through ten leagues from Pebble to Moon. Add friends with your code.

Streaks with rest days

Reach your daily goal and your streak grows. Miss a day and a rest day keeps it alive.

100 badges

From your first thousand steps to marathons, from sunrise walks to long streaks. They open up as you walk.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

Steps come from Apple Health, with your Apple Watch included and every step counted once. No need to keep the app open.

Free to walk with

Your journey, your streak and the first three postcards of every world are free, and you can watch the league board anytime.

Private by design

No ads, no location and no sign-up. Your step history stays on your iPhone. The league keeps only the name you choose, your region and this week's step totals, under an anonymous account.
