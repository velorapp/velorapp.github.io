Meet Cloy

A glossy drop of syrup with a honeyed voice. “You deserve it.” “Just one.” The sweet talk you've heard for years, finally out loud.

Seven short chapters

One a day, about 53 minutes in all. Each one takes apart something sugar tells you: that it's a treat, that it gives you energy, that everyone's eating it. Then comes your Last Bite.

When Cloy is talking

A craving hits? Name the lie, read the truth and ride it out with a 90-second breathing timer.

Ingredient X-Ray

Point your camera at an ingredients list and sugar's other names light up. It all happens on your iPhone.

Party Mode and Holiday Shield

A plan for tonight's party, and one for the holidays ahead.

Days that add up

Your sugar-free days add up, and a slip never resets your count. A quick question each evening, and widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Free for everyone, no subscription needed

The opening, the first chapter and help when a craving hits are open to everyone.

Private by design

No sign-up and no account. Your answers, check-ins and slips stay on your iPhone. Anonymous usage and crash reports, which you can turn off, help us fix and improve the app.
