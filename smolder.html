Quit by seeing through it

Smolder has an answer for everything. You'll have better ones.

Meet Smolder

The little con artist behind every craving. He has been talking you into it for years. Now you hear his pitch out loud, and see through it.

Your own order

A few honest questions about how you use nicotine, what you think it gives you and what worries you about stopping. Smolder's lies are ordered by how tightly each one holds you.

Short daily chapters

Eleven chapters over about eight days. Each one takes apart a single lie with a story, a picture and a question, not a lecture.

Not a willpower contest

You don't have to stop on day one. Keep using nicotine as usual while you read. The plan ends with one last cigarette, vape or pouch when you reach the final chapter.

See what it costs

Smolder's bill: the money, the time and the count, worked out from your own answers.

Every kind of nicotine

Cigarettes, rolling tobacco, vapes, heated tobacco, cigars and nicotine pouches.

Free for everyone, no subscription needed

The first chapter, and links to free stop-smoking helplines and services, are open to everyone.

Private by design

No ads and no tracking. Your answers and your plan stay on your iPhone. A random account ID keeps your subscription, and anonymous usage and crash reports help us fix and improve the app.
