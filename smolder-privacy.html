Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Smolder is an iPhone app that helps you quit nicotine by taking apart the beliefs that keep you using it. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Your answers and your plan stay on your iPhone. We never receive what you use, how much, what worries you or why you want to stop. To run the app we use Firebase (anonymous usage events and crash reports, which you can turn off) and RevenueCat (subscription records). Apple handles payments. There is no advertising, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Data on your iPhone

Your answers (which nicotine products you use, how much and for how long, what you spend, your past attempts to quit, what you feel nicotine gives you, what worries you about stopping, the thoughts you recognize, your reasons and anything you type in your own words), the plan built from them and your progress are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's protected storage. They are never sent to us or to any service.

This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

No account

Smolder has no sign-in and no account, and we keep no database about you. Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so you can restore it on a new iPhone from the app. If we add features that need more, such as connecting with friends, we will update this policy before they launch.

Usage and crash data

Firebase Analytics (Google) receives events about how the app is used: which onboarding step you reach, whether you allowed reminders, when the subscription screen is shown or closed and which plan you pick, purchases and restores, when you start or finish a chapter, an experiment or the ceremony, when you use one of the craving games or tell the app you had one, and when you open the app from a notification or a link. It also collects standard information such as your device model, iOS and app version, an app-instance identifier and your approximate region based on your IP address. These events never include your answers or anything you type.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports and error logs with similar device information, so we can find and fix problems.

Your choice. In the EU and the EEA, usage and crash data stay off until you say yes. In the UK and Switzerland the app tells you about them first, and you can turn them off. Everywhere, you can change your mind at any time in the app's Settings under Share anonymous usage data; while it's off, nothing is sent to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

We don't use the advertising identifier, don't ask to track you and don't use this data for advertising. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

To unlock Smolder Pro and to understand how subscriptions are doing, the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with a random ID that RevenueCat creates for the app on your iPhone. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email or your answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your iPhone, including one two days before a free trial ends. They are created on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

Deleting your data

In the app's Settings, Erase all my data deletes everything Smolder stored on your iPhone and resets the random identifiers used for usage data and purchases. Deleting the app also removes the data on your iPhone. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account and can be restored. To delete the purchase records RevenueCat holds, write to velorappsupport@gmail.com with the order ID from Apple's receipt email, so we can find them. We'll confirm when it's done.

Children

Smolder is for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.

Contact
