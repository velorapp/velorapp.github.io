Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Smolder is an iPhone app that helps you quit nicotine by taking apart the beliefs that keep you using it. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Your answers and your plan stay on your iPhone. We never receive what you use, how much, what worries you or why you want to stop. To run the app we use Firebase (a random account ID, app usage events and crash reports) and RevenueCat (subscription records). Apple handles payments. There is no advertising, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Data on your iPhone

Your answers (which nicotine products you use, how much and for how long, what you spend, your past attempts to quit, what you feel nicotine gives you, what worries you about stopping, the thoughts you recognize, your reasons and anything you type in your own words), the plan built from them and your progress are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's protected storage. They are never sent to us or to any service.

This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your anonymous account

When you first open Smolder, the app creates an anonymous account with Firebase Authentication, a Google service. It has no name, email or phone number, only a random ID. Our database (Cloud Firestore, also Google) stores that ID, when the account was created and that you use iOS. We use it to keep your subscription linked to you. If we add features that need more, such as connecting with friends, we will update this policy before they launch.

Usage and crash data

Firebase Analytics (Google) receives events about how the app is used: which onboarding step you reach, whether you allowed reminders, whether the subscription screen was shown or closed, and whether you subscribed and to which plan. It also collects standard information such as your device model, iOS and app version, an app-instance identifier and your approximate region based on your IP address. These events never include your answers.

Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports and error logs with similar device information, so we can find and fix problems.

We don't use the advertising identifier, don't ask to track you and don't use this data for advertising. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

To unlock Smolder Pro and to understand how subscriptions are doing, the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with your random account ID. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email or your answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your iPhone, including one two days before a free trial ends. They are created on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. To delete your anonymous account and the records linked to it at Firebase and RevenueCat, write to velorappsupport@gmail.com. Because the account has no name or email, include the order ID from Apple's receipt email if you subscribed, so we can find it. We'll confirm when it's done.

Children

Smolder is for adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to velorappsupport@gmail.com. Smolder is made by Velorapp, the studio name of Mertcan Erbasi.
