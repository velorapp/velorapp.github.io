Free help to quit

  • In the US: call 1-800-QUIT-NOW (1-800-784-8669), free, or visit smokefree.gov.
  • In England: call the NHS Smokefree National Helpline on 0300 123 1044, free, or visit NHS Quit Smoking.
  • Elsewhere: ask a doctor or pharmacist about free stop-smoking services where you live.
  • If you have chest pain or trouble breathing, call your local emergency number.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Smolder and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick Smolder. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all payments and refunds.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 3 days free. If notifications are on, Smolder reminds you two days before the trial ends. You won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. The monthly plan has no trial.

Do I have to stop on the first day?

No. Keep using nicotine as usual while you go through the chapters. The plan ends with one last cigarette, vape or pouch when you reach the final chapter.

Where is my data? Can I move it to another phone?

Your answers and your plan stay on your iPhone, so we can't see, recover or move them. They are included in your iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along.

My reminders don't arrive.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications > Smolder and make sure notifications are allowed.

Is Smolder a medical treatment?

No. Smolder is a habit-change app, not medical advice or treatment. If you are pregnant, have a health condition or take medication, talk to a doctor or pharmacist about quitting and about stop-smoking medicines.
