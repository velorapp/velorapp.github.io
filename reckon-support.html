Common questions

How does a day work?

In the morning, write up to three vows on the mirror and hold the button to sign them. From your evening hour on, the mirror asks about each one: kept or not. Answering them all closes the day. You choose both hours in Settings.

It’s past midnight. Can I still answer for today?

Yes. A day runs until four in the morning, so a night that goes on past midnight is still that day. At four the next day begins.

What if I didn’t keep a vow?

Say so. Answering “I didn’t” still closes the day and keeps your run going. The vow you didn’t keep comes along to the next day.

Why is the mirror fogged?

A day you signed and never answered for, or a day you didn’t come at all, fogs the mirror. Wipe it to face the days you missed and start again. Before your first signature nothing is held against you.

The mirror is dark.

Reckon needs the camera to be a mirror. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps > Reckon and turn on Camera. Nothing is recorded, and the picture never leaves your phone.

How does the ram grow?

Every vow you keep adds a ring to Koç’s horns. Kept vows move you through seven ranks, from Recruit to Unbroken, and after that there is a new mark at every five hundred.

Can I add a vow with Siri?

Yes. Say “Give my word in Reckon” and tell Siri the vow. It goes on the mirror; you still sign it there, by hand, looking at yourself.

How does the free trial work?

The weekly plan starts with 3 days free for new subscribers. If notifications are on, Reckon reminds you the day before the trial ends. You won’t be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. The yearly plan has no trial.

How do I cancel?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick Reckon. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all payments and refunds.

I have a new iPhone.

Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud: Reckon brings your days back from your iCloud copy when you open it. To unlock Reckon Pro again, tap Restore at the bottom of the plans screen or in Settings.

My notifications don’t arrive.

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications > Reckon and make sure notifications are allowed. Reckon never shows what you wrote on the lock screen.

Where is my data?

Your vows and your days stay on your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud. The privacy policy has the details.

Is Reckon advice?

No. Reckon is a tool for keeping your own word, not medical or psychological advice or treatment. If you are struggling, talk to someone you trust or a professional.
