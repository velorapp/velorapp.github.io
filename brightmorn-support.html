Common questions

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Brightmorn and tap Settings > Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore at the bottom of the subscription screen.

How do I cancel?

In Brightmorn, tap Settings > Manage subscription. Or open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all payments and refunds.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. You won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. The monthly plan has no trial.

Where is my data? Can I move it to another phone?

Your plan and logged days stay on your iPhone. There is no account and nothing is stored on our side, so we can't recover or move it for you. It is included in your iCloud or computer backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings it along.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or +, then search for Brightmorn. The Today widget lets you log an alcohol-free day without opening the app.

My reminders don't arrive.

Check-ins come only on days you haven't logged yet. If nothing arrives, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications > Brightmorn and make sure notifications are allowed.

Can I hide the word "alcohol" on my Lock Screen?

Yes. In Brightmorn, open Settings and turn on discreet mode under Privacy. Notifications, widgets and the Lock Screen then say "good days" instead.

How do I start over?

To change your goal or start date, tap Settings > Your plan. To erase everything, tap Settings > Reset all data. Resetting doesn't affect your subscription.
