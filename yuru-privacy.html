Yuru

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Yuru is an iPhone app that turns your steps into a journey: a wolf that walks as far as you do, postcards from the places you reach and a weekly league. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Your step history is read from Apple Health and your iPhone's motion sensor, and it stays on your iPhone. To run the weekly league we keep, under an anonymous account with no email and no password, the name you choose, your country or region as set on your iPhone and your step total for each day of the current week. To run the app we use Firebase (the league and anonymous usage events) and RevenueCat (subscription records). Apple handles payments. There is no advertising, and the app doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Health and motion data

With your permission, Yuru reads three things from Apple Health: your steps, the distance you walk and the flights of stairs you climb. It also counts steps with your iPhone's motion sensor (Motion & Fitness), so the wolf moves while you walk. Yuru never writes anything to Apple Health.

Your history and everything built from it (your journey, postcards, badges, streak and daily goal) are stored on your iPhone. The only step figures that leave it are the daily totals of the current league week, described below. Health and motion data is never used for advertising, never sold and never given to data brokers.

What is stored on your iPhone is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes it.

The league and your name

The first time you open Yuru it creates an anonymous account: a random ID, with no email, phone number or password. Under that ID our database (Firebase, run by Google and hosted in the European Union) keeps:

  • the name you choose and the short code that goes with it, for example Amber Finch #GL39;
  • your country or region, taken from your iPhone's region setting, not from where you are;
  • your step total for each day of the current league week, from which the server works out your XP;
  • your wolf's stage, your league and your weekly results;
  • the friends you add, by their name and code.

Other walkers see your name, its code, your wolf's stage and your weekly XP: in your league group, on the boards of friends who added you and on the country and world boards. They never see your step history. The name doesn't have to be your real one, and names are checked automatically for unkind words.

If you would rather walk alone, choose Walk without the league on the League tab. Your row then comes off the week's board.

No location

Yuru never asks for your location. The places on the journey belong to the story; they are not where you are.

Usage data

Firebase Analytics (Google) receives events about how the app is used: which welcome step you reach, the daily goal you set, whether you connected Apple Health and allowed notifications, when the subscription screen is shown or closed, trials, purchases and restores, when you reach your goal, a stop, a new world, a level or a badge, rest days and streaks, when you open or share a postcard, and what you do in the league (opening it, how a week ended, adding friends, sharing your code, walking without it). It also collects standard information such as your device model, iOS and app version, an app-instance identifier and your approximate region based on your IP address. Events are tied to your anonymous account ID. They never include your step counts, your name or anything you type. If your iPhone's region is set to a country in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, usage analytics is switched off and none of these events are sent.

We don't use the advertising identifier, don't ask to track you and don't use this data for advertising. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

To unlock Yuru Pro and to understand how subscriptions are doing, the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with your anonymous account ID. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email or your steps. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules them on your iPhone: when you reach your daily goal or a new stop, when your streak needs a few more steps, when your league week is about to close and how it ended, and one before a free trial ends. They are created on your device; nothing about them is sent to us.

Where your data is kept

The league database is hosted by Google in the European Union. Google and RevenueCat may also process data in the United States and other countries, under their own privacy terms linked above.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything Yuru stored on your iPhone. To delete your league record (your name, code, results and friends), your usage data and the purchase records RevenueCat holds, write to velorappsupport@gmail.com with your Yuru name and code, so we can find them. We'll confirm when it's done. Your subscription stays with your Apple Account, where you can cancel it at any time.

Children

Yuru is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
