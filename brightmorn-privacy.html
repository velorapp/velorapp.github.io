Brightmorn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Brightmorn is an iPhone app that helps you track alcohol-free days. This policy explains what happens to your information.

The short version

Brightmorn does not collect, store or share your plan, your logged days or your answers. We don't have servers for the app. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only outside service is RevenueCat, which shows us subscription statistics (see Purchases).

Data on your iPhone

Your plan, the days you log, what you note came before a drink, your "next time" plan, your reminder settings and your starting numbers (drinks and spending per week) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widgets.

If you add "my person", Brightmorn keeps only the name and phone number you pick, on your iPhone. It never reads the rest of your contacts and never sends a message by itself.

This data is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your own backup settings. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it anytime in Settings > Reset all data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether your subscription is active so it can unlock features. See Apple's privacy policy.

To understand how subscriptions are doing (for example, how many free trials turn into paid subscriptions), the app shares your App Store purchase records with RevenueCat, together with a random identifier created for the app. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your email, your plan, your logged days or your answers. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

Brightmorn is not intended for children and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: write to the address on our Support page. Brightmorn is made by Velorapp, the studio name of Mertcan Erbasi.
