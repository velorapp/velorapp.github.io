Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Velorapp builds apps that keep your information on your device. This page covers this website and how we handle messages you send us. Each app has its own policy with the details for that app.

App privacy policies

  • Brightmorn Privacy Policy

This website

This website has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser requests them.

Messages you send us

When you email us, we use your email address and what you write only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. We don't add you to mailing lists and don't share your message with anyone. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

Children

Our apps and this website are not directed at children under 13, and some apps are meant for adults only, as their App Store age rating shows.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Because our apps keep your data on your device, there is usually nothing on our side except emails you sent us. Write to us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and this page.
