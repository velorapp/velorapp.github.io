Brightmorn

Drink less, one day at a time. Track alcohol-free days, see what you gain and get through cravings.

Coming soon to the App StoreHealth & FitnessiPhone · English · Español

Everything in one calm place

Your sky warms as your alcohol-free days add up.

Choose your goal

  • An alcohol-free challenge of 14 to 90 days
  • Stop for good, one day at a time
  • A number of alcohol-free days each week
  • Cut back with a weekly drink limit

Log in one tap

Mark a day alcohol-free, or note how many drinks you had. No judgment: every alcohol-free day counts, even after a slip.

See what you gain

Estimates of the money you keep, the drinks you skip and the calories you avoid, based on your own starting point.

Home Screen and Lock Screen

Log today from the widget without opening the app. Lock Screen widgets show your count at a glance.

When it's hard

Add your person and text or call them in one tap. After a slip, note what came before and plan your next time.

Stay on track

A gentle evening check-in only on days you haven't logged, a calendar of your month, and milestones from day 1 to day 365.

Free for everyone, no subscription needed

The 15-minute craving timer with breathing and ideas, support resources for your country, and a guide to stopping safely after heavy drinking.

Private by design

No account and no tracking. Your plan and your days stay on your iPhone. Discreet mode keeps the word "alcohol" off your Lock Screen and notifications.
