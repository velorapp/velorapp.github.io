Reckon

Give your word to the one person you can’t fool. Your front camera becomes a mirror: write today’s vows on it in the morning and answer for each one at night.

Coming soon to the App StoreiPhone · English · Español · Português · Türkçe

Say it. Sign it. Answer for it.

One mirror, three notes and a ram. No feed, no ads, no account.

A mirror, not a feed

Your front camera turns the screen into a mirror, and you write your vows looking at yourself. Nothing is recorded; the picture never leaves your iPhone.

Your word in the morning

Up to three vows a day, each on a yellow note stuck to the glass. A firm hold signs them. After that they are not plans any more, they are promises.

Your answer at night

In the evening the mirror asks about each vow: kept or not. The night still counts until four in the morning, so a late answer is still an answer.

Honesty keeps the run

Saying “I didn’t” still closes the day. Only not showing up breaks your run, and a missed day fogs the mirror until you wipe it.

A ram that earns his horns

Every vow you keep adds a ring to Koç’s horns. Seven ranks, from Recruit to Unbroken, and marks that keep going long after that.

The mirror calls

A call at the morning hour you choose and a question in the evening. Your vows never appear on the lock screen.

Look around first

The mirror, writing your vows, your proof and your ram are open to everyone. Signing a vow, the moment you give your word, takes Reckon Pro.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up and no ads. Your vows and your days stay on your iPhone, with a copy in your own iCloud so a new phone starts where you left off. The camera is only a mirror: nothing is recorded, and the picture never leaves your phone.
