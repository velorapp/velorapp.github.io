Billing

Cancel a subscription

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and pick the app. Most of our apps also have a Manage subscription button in their settings.

Ask for a refund

Apple handles all App Store payments, so we can't issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe, open the app and tap Restore purchases in its settings.
